Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

We bake in small batches every morning. Prices below are typical counter ranges—no online checkout, so just swing by or send a pre-order request on our contact page.

Breads

  • Seeded Multigrain & Sandwich Loaves: Made with toasted oats, flax, and sunflower seeds for everyday lunches ($7 – $9).
  • Ciabatta & Dinner Rolls: Light crusts and soft interiors for soups and family meals ($5 – $8).

Pastries

Cakes & Sweet Treats

  • Celebration & Event Cakes: Custom-sized cakes made with advance notice for birthdays and local gatherings ($38 – $75).
