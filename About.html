About North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery started with a simple idea: bake real, dependable bread and morning pastries close to home using unhurried fermentation and straight-forward ingredients.

Our Story

After years of baking for friends, family tables, and local pop-ups, we opened our neighborhood shop to give people a reliable spot for warm loaves and morning coffee. Every batch starts early in the quiet hours before the street wakes up.

How We Bake

We focus on slow dough fermentation, real butter, unbleached flour, and seasonal fruit rather than shortcuts or heavy preservatives. Some days we sell out of a favorite pastry early, and we treat that as a sign to keep our batches honest and fresh.
